# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is CEOs, founders, and leadership teams in Vietnamese companies that are trying to grow with clearer strategy, stronger operating rhythm, and more practical use of AI. They come to the site looking for evidence that Thanh Tung can think at executive level, build real systems, and help teams turn ambition into execution.

The secondary audience is people learning AI, product, and engineering. For them, the site should feel like a high-signal learning surface from an operator who has built SaaS, AI agents, industrial IoT platforms, and education at scale.

## Product Purpose

This is Thanh Tung / David Tung's personal business website. It exists to attract potential partners and clients, showcase real projects and services, and publish practical thinking on management, operations, AI, product architecture, and digital transformation.

Success means a qualified visitor quickly understands the range of expertise, trusts the proof points, and sees the site as a doorway into strategic advisory, AI/product coaching, and serious implementation work rather than as a simple personal blog.

## Brand Personality

Modern, technological, and clear. The brand should feel like a contemporary agency combined with premium executive coaching: sharp, structured, visually distinctive, and grounded in real operating experience.

The visual voice can use monochrome patterns, optical rhythm, motion, and strong information architecture to create a memorable technical feel. The tone should stay direct and practical, with enough ambition to signal high-end advisory work.

## Anti-references

This should not look like a pure personal blog, a generic corporate consultant site, or a soft SaaS landing page filled with template cards and vague claims.

Avoid designs that feel only editorial, only minimal, or only content-first. The site should preserve the personal voice while presenting services, projects, and thinking with the confidence of a modern agency.

## Design Principles

Practice through proof: lead with real projects, measurable outcomes, and concrete operating experience before making broad claims.

Personal but commercial: keep the author's point of view visible, while making the path toward partnerships, clients, and coaching clear.

Systems over decoration: use visual patterns, motion, and structure to express strategy, execution, and technical depth rather than adding ornament for its own sake.

Clarity at executive speed: make pages easy for busy leaders to scan, compare, and trust without flattening the brand into a plain blog.

Teach from the field: make AI, product, and engineering content feel practical, opinionated, and useful for people who want to apply it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast, keyboard focus, navigation, and responsive behavior. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences, especially for animated backgrounds and optical patterns. Bilingual Vietnamese and English content should remain first-class, with route structure and locale switching preserved.
