## Where the AI Agent market stands

2025–2026 marks the shift from AI answering questions to AI doing work: self-planning, using tools, running to completion. The difference is what you delegate. With a chatbot, you give a question and get text back. With an agent, you give an outcome — a report sent, data entered, a website deployed — and it works through multiple steps to get there.

## Why Hermes Agent is the easiest starting point

Of the four tools, Hermes is the only one designed to live outside a developer's screen. It runs persistently on your machine or VPS, and you talk to it through a messaging app. You don't need to learn a new interface, don't need an IDE, and everything is done in natural language.

## Practical principle

Pick one process you do manually each week that takes 2–5 hours and has clear pass/fail criteria. That's the first thing to delegate to an agent.
